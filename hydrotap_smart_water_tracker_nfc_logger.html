Mobile File Restriction Note

Android/iOS browsers block opening file:// links directly when tapped from NFC tags due to OS security. For local files, trigger via Tasker/MacroDroid or host on free Netlify/GitHub Pages!

How to Write Tag with "NFC Tools" App

1
Copy the Link Above

Click the "Copy Link" button above to get your custom parameters.

2
Open NFC Tools App

Open the free NFC Tools app on Android or iOS.

3
Write URL Record

Tap Write → Add a record → URL / URI. Paste your link and hit OK.

4
Tap Your Tag

Tap Write in the app, then hold your NFC sticker against the back of your phone to program it!
